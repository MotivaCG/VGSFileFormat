= VGS Capture =

"""Plays a VGS 4D Gaussian splat capture (.vgs, .pgs) as Houdini GSplats."""

Outputs the splats of a capture at the current time as points with the attributes
[Bake GSplats|Node:sop/bakegsplat] makes - `Cd`, `orient`, `scale`, `GS_Alpha`, and with
harmonics `GS_SPH_R`, `GS_SPH_G`, `GS_SPH_B` and `restorient` - so they draw in the
viewport and render in Karma XPU through Solaris. A detail attribute `vgs_seconds` holds the
capture time shown.

Inside a [VGS Capture object|Node:obj/vgs_capture], which File > Import creates, its
parameters follow the object's. On its own, in any SOP network or a SOP Create LOP, it is
driven by its own parameters.

With points wired into its input, or named in __Points__, it puts a copy of the capture on
every point, each at its own time; see Scatter in the
[VGS Capture object|Node:obj/vgs_capture]'s help.

The parameters are those of the [VGS Capture object|Node:obj/vgs_capture].
